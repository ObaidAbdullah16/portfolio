Method and limits

  • Read-only access: a cross-account role with the AWS-managed SecurityAudit and ViewOnlyAccess policies, a few extra read permissions, and an explicit deny on reading data (S3 objects, database rows, secret values, log events). The account's own CloudTrail records every call.
  • Engine: Prowler 5.47.0 (open source, Apache-2.0) running 48 curated checks, plus two native checks for spend alerts and CI/CD credentials. Each finding was reviewed by a person before delivery.
  • Score: each evaluated control counts by severity (critical 10, high 6, medium 3, low 1). Controls with no matching resources, or that could not be evaluated, are left out of the score and shown as coverage instead.
  • Findings reflect the configuration at the time of the scan. A baseline check is not a penetration test and not a guarantee that the account cannot be breached.
  • Not evaluated in the simulator (ID-01, ID-07, DA-01): it has no root user, IAM Access Analyzer or account-level S3 settings. A scan of a real account evaluates them.
