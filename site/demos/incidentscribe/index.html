IncidentScribe

Alert in → on-call summary out. Runbook-grounded, PII-redacted, cost-capped AI on Amazon Bedrock.
Live demo with synthetic incidents. Every request is redacted before it reaches the model, checked by a Bedrock Guardrail, and counted against a hard daily budget. When the budget is used up you still get a deterministic (non-AI) summary. Source: github.com/ObaidAbdullah16/incidentscribe-ai
